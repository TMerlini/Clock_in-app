Clock In

Intelligent time tracking for Portuguese labour law compliance.

Clock In is a work time-tracking application for freelancers, contractors and employees in Portugal. It records clock-in and clock-out times, separates regular hours from paid overtime and unpaid extra hours (Isenção de horário), tracks usage against the annual Isenção limit, and warns when hours approach the limits set by the Código do Trabalho.

What the app does

  • Track work sessions, breaks and locations, with weekend and bank-holiday rules
  • Categorise hours as regular, paid overtime or Isenção, and monitor the annual limit
  • Analytics and finance summaries, including weekend bonuses and days off earned
  • Optionally sync work sessions to Google Calendar, and import existing calendar events as work sessions
  • An AI Advisor that answers questions about your own recorded hours under Portuguese labour law
