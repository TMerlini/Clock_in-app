Privacy Policy

Clock In - Intelligent Time Manager | Last updated: 11 August 2026

1. Introduction

Clock In ("we", "our", "the app") is a time tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using Clock In, you agree to this policy and our Terms of Service.

2. Data We Collect

Account data: When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and profile information (name, profile picture) from Google.

Work sessions: Clock-in/out times, break durations, session notes, and related time tracking data you create.

Settings: Your preferences (hourly rate, tax settings, subscription plan, etc.) stored in the app.

Optional – Google Calendar: If you enable calendar sync, we create and update events in your Google Calendar based on your work sessions. We do not read other calendar events beyond what is necessary to sync.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the Clock In service: storing sessions, generating analytics, calculating finance summaries, syncing to Google Calendar (if enabled), and managing your subscription. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Google: Sign-in and optional Calendar sync. Google's privacy policy applies to data shared with Google: Google Privacy Policy.

Firebase (Google): We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore to store your account and session data securely.

Stripe: For paid subscriptions, payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store full payment card details.

OpenRouter: For the AI Advisor feature, we use OpenRouter for AI model routing. Conversation content may be processed according to their terms.

5. Google User Data and Limited Use

Clock In requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • openid, email, profile — to create and identify your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly — to list your calendars so you can choose which one work sessions are written to.
  • calendar.events — to create and update calendar events that correspond to your work sessions.

Calendar access is optional. The app is fully usable without it, and you can disconnect it at any time from within the app.

Clock In's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, we do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide the service or as required by law, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymised.

Google user data is not used to train generalised artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. The AI Advisor feature operates on your own session and finance data only, and never on Google Calendar content.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud). We use industry-standard security practices. You are responsible for keeping your Google account secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Retention. We retain your account and session data for as long as your account remains active, because the app's purpose is to keep a durable record of your work history. Google Calendar access tokens are short-lived and are refreshed or discarded on expiry; we do not retain them beyond their validity.

Deleting Google Calendar data. Disconnecting Calendar in the app removes the stored access token immediately. Events already written to your Google Calendar belong to your Google account and can be deleted by you directly in Google Calendar.

Deleting your account. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing contacto@clock-in.pt from the address associated with your account. We action deletion requests within 30 days, after which your session data, settings and stored tokens are permanently removed from our systems. You may export your data first from Settings → Data & Backup.

You may also revoke Clock In's access to your Google account at any time, independently of us, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Your Rights

You can access and export your data from within the app (Settings → Data & Backup). To request deletion of your account and data, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: contacto@clock-in.pt
